## KPIs to track weekly

| KPI | Target | Kill / fix threshold |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn acceptance | ≥25% | <15% → rewrite the connection note or retarget |
| Email reply rate (per template) | ≥2% | <0.5% after 200 sends → rewrite subject and first line |
| Hard bounce rate / inbox / day | <2% | >3% → pause the inbox |
| Spam rate (Postmaster) | <0.1% | ≥0.3% → stop all sends |
| Positive replies → calls | ≥40% | <25% → fix the reply playbook |
| Calls → pilot | ≥20% | <10% → review pricing and the offer |
| Time to reply to a prospect | <2 business hours | — |

## Daily 60-minute routine

1. **15 min:** reply to every inbound reply.
2. **20 min:** send 15 LinkedIn connection requests, 5 DMs to new accepts, 3 real comments.
3. **10 min:** check the approvals queue, bounces and suppression list.
4. **15 min:** research tomorrow's 15 leads: open each lead's source link and rewrite the buying signal in your own words.
